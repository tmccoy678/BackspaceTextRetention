# Foundation evidence

The repository's primary study is the prospective blinded ten-card recovery
challenge described in the [main manuscript-style README](../README.md).

The material here preserves the completed observation that motivated that
study. It establishes a technically plausible transcript-recovery path, but it
is not an outcome from the prospective card challenge:

- [Original observation and completed technical case report](original-observation/)
- [Byte-preserved transcripts](../evidence/transcripts/)
- [Contemporaneous screenshots](../evidence/screenshots/)
- [Original diagnostic protocol](../docs/EXPERIMENT-PROTOCOL.md)
- [Original diagnostic results](../docs/EXPERIMENT-RESULTS.md)
- [Claim boundaries and corrections](../docs/FINDINGS.md)
- [Evidence timeline](../docs/TIMELINE.md)

Keeping the foundation separate prevents the completed retrospective case from
being mistaken for confirmatory evidence from the not-yet-run blinded study.
